---
title: "Activating your agent"
---

## **When am I ready?**

When three things are true:

- Your main protocols are published and set to Active.
- Your integrations are connected and tested.
- Your subscription is running.

You don’t need everything in place. If your largest categories are covered, usually order status, delivery and returns, you’re ready.

<Warning>
  Don’t go live with an empty Training Center. Your team will see suggestions that make no sense, and that impression is hard to correct.
</Warning>

## How do I activate the agent?

Go to [Dashboard \> Agents](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/agents), open the agent, set it to [active](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/agents), and update. Minimal needs an active subscription, see how to start the subscription in this article.

## Will it start emailing my customers?

Not unless you’ve enabled automation. With global automation off, every reply is left as a **suggestion** for a human to review and send. Nothing reaches a customer without someone clicking send.

## What else should I know before I switch it on?

- It works per agent, so you can take one brand or country live and leave the others paused.
- It only picks up messages that arrive while it’s active. Your existing queue stays with your team. You can set it back to paused at any time.
- A reply typically appears within a minute or two, depending on ticket complexity, your configured response delay, and how quickly your helpdesk notifies us.

## What appears in my helpdesk?

Two internal notes on each ticket: the drafted reply, and directly above it a note linking to the **insights page**.

If the draft is good, copy it into your reply and send. You don’t need to mark it as good, directly-copied suggestions are measured automatically. If it’s wrong, open the insights page and leave feedback there.

## What should I expect in the first week?

Mixed results, and that’s normal. Your Training Center covers most of what’s needed at this point, but it’s the detail and the exceptions that customers actually ask about.

You’ll see three patterns:

- Subjects handled well straight away, usually order status and tracking.
- Subjects where one detail is consistently missing from the protocol.
- Tickets where the agent escalates because the protocol has no instruction for that case. That’s correct behaviour, not a failure.

<Note>
  A reply along the lines of “I’ll check with the team and get back to you” almost always means the agent has no information for that scenario. It won’t invent an answer, so a holding reply is all that’s left to it. Treat it as a flag that a protocol is missing or wasn’t matched.
</Note>

## Who should have access?

Invite your team before you go live. Two roles matter:

- **Support agents**: can give feedback on tickets, nothing else.=
- **Admins**: can see and change protocols.

The usual setup is the whole support team giving feedback and one admin processing it.

## Where do I see how it’s performing?

On the [Analytics page](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/usage-performance). In the early weeks watch your direct resolution rate rather than your automation rate, and look at the trend across weeks rather than the daily number.
