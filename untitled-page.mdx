Thank you for joining Minimal! Starting with Minimal is very easy, to give some perspective on how to become succesful with Minimal fast, we have written out a project plan below on what you need to do to start automating your customer support.

# The project plan

Everybody does the project on there own timeframe. With weekly meetings with Minimal we see 4 weeks as easy doable. Some partners do it in one week, some in 8. This depends on allocated time and resources.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Week 1">
    Focus:Setting up integrations, getting to know the platform & writing first protocols

    Outcome: All relevant integrations are added & main categories are covered and published
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 2">
    Focus: Activate the agent \+ writing/processing feedback

    Outcome: Suggestions arrive in your helpdesk and your team reviews them
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 3">
    Focus: Keep processing feedback \+ enable automation

    Outcome: First protocols send replies on their own
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 4">
    Focus: Broaden automation \+ add actions

    Outcome: The agent starts executing changes in your systems
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Each phase builds on the previous one. Going live without filled protocols produces poor suggestions, and a team that sees poor suggestions in week one is hard to win back.
</Info>

## What to do \+ tips in the weeks

<Accordion title="Week 1: Integrations + write protocols">
  1. **Appoint one owner.** Someone who knows the customer service well and isn’t buried in daily tickets. Involve someone who does handle tickets daily too — they know the exceptions.
  2. **Cover your biggest categories first.** Usually order status and tracking, delivery, and returns. You don’t need everything before you go live.
  3. **Test in the Playground.** Use a real customer name and email address, otherwise the agent can’t perform a real lookup and you won’t see what it will actually do.

  Three things also need arranging this week, and they have lead time:

  - **Any missing integrations.** Usually an API key that has to come from an external party. Without a connection the agent can only give FAQ answers.
  - **Your mail flow.** If customer service runs partly through a general info address, route everything through your helpdesk. The agent only sees what arrives there.
  - **Your subscription.** Needed before you can use the Playground or set the agent to active.

  See Writing protocols with the AI Manager.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Week 2: Activate agent and start the feedback loop">
  **Set the agent to active.** It runs in suggestion mode, so it drafts replies your team reviews and sends, nothing reaches a customer without someone clicking send.

  **Invite your team now.** Colleagues who only need to give feedback get support agent access, not admin, so they can flag what’s wrong without changing protocols.

  **Give feedback via the insights page: **The feedback loop is what the next two weeks are really about. The first jump in quality comes from protocols. The last stretch — the details that make answers correct every time — comes almost entirely from feedback.

  Read how to give feedback via this article.

  <Warning>
    Giving feedback is the key to fast success. Even fifteen minutes a day of giving feedback helps getting Minimal to 80% automation fast.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Week 3: Start automating">
  When a subject shows consistently good suggestions, you can let the agent send those replies on its own. Start with simple, high-volume, low-risk scenarios and add one or two at a time.

  Watch your **direct resolution rate** rather than your automation rate. It tells you whether the answers are good enough yet, and it’s what makes automation safe to switch on.

  Read how to start with automation in this article
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Week 4: Add actions">
  Actions let the agent execute changes in your systems rather than only replying. Examples are: cancelling an order, updating an address, creating a return label.

  Actions are configured for your organisation by Minimal, so raise which ones you want early. Write access in your source system is usually needed, and that often has to go through your developer or agency.

  Read how to start with Actions in this article
</Accordion>

## What to expect from Minimal?

Minimal is always open for support and answering questions. We plan a weekly session of in the first period, dropping in frequency after that. If you run several brands we can schedule separately per brand.

In between, use the Cases function in the AI Manager to raise anything we need to pick up — the ticket and context are attached automatically.

Direct communication is always possible via:

- Slack Channel
- Microsoft Teams chat
- Email
- Phone

## What separates the fast clients from the slow ones?

- One clear owner for the protocols and the feedback queue.
- Fixed time in the calendar. Fifteen minutes a day is enough, but it has to be every day.
- Internal alignment. Where your team disagrees on how something should be handled, the agent won’t be consistent either.
