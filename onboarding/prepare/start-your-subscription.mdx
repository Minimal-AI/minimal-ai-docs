## Overview

You can connect systems and write protocols straight away. The Playground and live suggestions in your helpdesk need an active subscription. Start it early: testing and real feedback improve the agent faster than anything else, and you pay nothing extra while the agent is paused.

**What you achieve:** an active subscription, so nothing holds you up in the Onboard and Feedback steps.

**Time needed:** about 2 minutes.

## What needs a subscription

| Available straight away | Needs an active subscription |
| --- | --- |
| Integrations | The [Playground](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/playground) |
| Agents and their settings | Suggestions and automation in your helpdesk |
| The Training Center and the [AI Manager](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/ai-manager) | Processing feedback |
| | Agent performance and analytics |

## Before you start

- A colleague with billing rights and a payment method.
- A rough idea of your monthly ticket volume. If you don't know it, start small and adjust later.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan & Billing">
    Go to [Plan & Billing](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/billing) in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your number of tickets">
    Choose the number of billable tickets per month and click **Get started**. What counts as a billable ticket is explained under [Usage & Performance](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/usage-performance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and pay">
    Click **Confirm** and complete the payment. The subscription is active straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Starting a subscription does not switch anything on in your helpdesk. Your agent stays **paused** until you set it to **active** yourself in [step 3](/onboarding/feedback/go-live-in-suggestion-mode). While it is paused, no tickets are used.
</Note>

## Good to know

- **Testing is free.** Tickets in the Playground don't count towards your subscription.
- **Tickets are only used once the agent is active** and connected to your helpdesk.
- **Your period runs from your start date**, not per calendar month. Unused tickets do not carry over to the next period.
- **When you reach your limit**, the agent pauses. You can buy extra tickets or upgrade to a larger plan.
- **Higher volume costs less per ticket.** You can move to a larger plan at any time. For a yearly plan, contact the Minimal team.

<Check>
  **Done when:** the subscription warning is gone from your dashboard and you can start a session in the [Playground](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/playground).
</Check>
