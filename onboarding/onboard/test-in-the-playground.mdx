## Overview

The [Playground](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/playground) shows what the agent would write in your helpdesk, without touching customers. It uses your real integrations, so you see exactly how it looks up orders and follows your protocols. Every reply comes with an **insights page**, the same page your team uses for feedback once the agent is live.

**What you achieve:** confidence that your main topics get sensible answers, and familiarity with the insights page.

**Time needed:** about 15 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your subscription is active. See [Start your subscription](/onboarding/prepare/start-your-subscription).
- Your first protocols are published.
- Your webshop has finished indexing.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a session">
    Open the [Playground](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/playground), start a new session and select your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask as a real customer">
    Enter the name and email address of a real customer with a real order, and type the question the way that customer would. The agent then searches your actual systems. Copying a few real tickets from your helpdesk works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the insights page">
    Below the reply, open the insights page. It shows the reply, the explanation behind it (which protocols it used and which systems it checked) and an [AI Manager](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/ai-manager) loaded with this one reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve what is wrong">
    Ask the AI Manager *why* it answered like this, or tell it what should be different: *"We never open with Hi"*, *"Use the date from the returns portal"*. It proposes a change to a protocol, your tone of voice or a rule. Review it and save or publish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test again">
    Start a new session and ask the same question. The next reply already uses your change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Testing in the Playground does not use tickets. A reply can take around half a minute, which is slower than in your helpdesk. Judge the content, not the speed.
</Info>

<Check>
  **Done when:**

  - Each of your main topics has had at least one realistic test.
  - You have improved at least one reply through the insights page and seen the result.
</Check>

## Common pitfalls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Testing with a made-up customer">
    Without a real name, email address and order, the agent cannot do a lookup. It will ask for an order number, and you won't see what it will actually do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Testing for too long">
    The Playground is for checking your main topics, not for perfecting every reply. Real tickets give you many more examples every day. Once your main topics look reasonable, go live in suggestion mode.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Testing helpdesk actions">
    Tags, internal notes and ticket fields cannot run in the Playground. Test those on a real ticket in suggestion mode.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
