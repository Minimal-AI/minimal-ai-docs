## Overview

A protocol is a step plan for one topic: what the agent checks, in which system, and what it does in each situation. You don't write it alone. You explain the topic to the [AI Manager](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/ai-manager) as you would to a new colleague, it asks follow up questions, and it turns your explanation into a protocol in the Training Center.

**What you achieve:** published protocols for your five to ten most common topics.

**Time needed:** about an hour for the first five protocols. Most teams end up with about twenty.

## Before you start

- Your systems are connected and a test lookup works. Protocols written without integrations stay vague.
- You have your list of top topics and the documents from [Prepare your team](/onboarding/prepare/prepare-your-team).
- Your Training Center already has standard e-commerce categories, such as returns, order status and complaints. You fill them with protocols.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new conversation per topic">
    Open the [AI Manager](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/ai-manager), click **New conversation** and type, for example: *"Help me write a protocol about returns."* Start a new conversation for every topic, so earlier messages don't get mixed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain the process like you would to a new colleague">
    Describe the steps your team takes, in Dutch or English. Three sentences or four pages both work. You can paste an existing work instruction, attach a PDF, point to a page on your website or dictate with the microphone.

    Explain the reasoning, not the reply text: *"Look up the order in Shopify. If it was delivered less than 30 days ago, send the link to our returns portal. If it is older, explain that it can no longer be returned. If the product is damaged, ask for a photo and hand the ticket to the team."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the follow up questions">
    The AI Manager asks when a step has no outcome, and tells you when a topic overlaps or conflicts with an existing protocol. These questions show where your own process has gaps. Answer them as concretely as you can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Click **Review protocol**, check the proposal and click **Publish** at the bottom right. The protocol is saved when the button turns dark grey and reads **Published**. An unpublished protocol is not used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for your top topics">
    Work through your list, most common topic first. Ask the AI Manager which topics are still missing when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes a good protocol

- **Steps as "if this, then that".** Every branch has an outcome, including when to hand over to your team.
- **Every system named.** *"If the order was fulfilled more than 4 days ago according to Shopify"* is clear. *"Check the order"* is not.
- **Processes, not answer templates.** The agent writes the reply itself, in your tone of voice. Macros and FAQ answers alone say what to tell the customer, not what happens behind it.
- **A clear scope.** Say when the protocol applies and when it does not, especially for topics that look alike.
- **Only promises you can keep.** The agent follows protocols literally. Don't write *"we will send a reset link"* if nobody or no action will.
- **Compact.** Related subtopics, such as size and washing advice, can share one protocol. Very long or strict protocols make the agent hand over more often.

The recommended categories and best practices are in [Protocols](/training/protocols).

<Tip>
  A good test: could a new colleague handle the ticket with only this protocol? If not, something is missing.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Done when:**

  - Your five to ten most common topics each have a published protocol.
  - Each protocol names the systems it uses and has an outcome for every branch.
</Check>

## Common pitfalls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Forgetting to publish">
    Nothing is saved automatically. Unpublished protocols are not used, and the work is easy to lose. Always check for the dark grey **Published** button.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Waiting until every topic is covered">
    You don't need everything before you go live. Covering your biggest topics is enough. Real tickets show what is missing, which is what step 3 is for.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Writing about a system that is not connected">
    If the AI Manager ignores details you gave it, a missing integration is the most likely reason. Connect the system and ask it to rewrite the protocol.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A protocol per product">
    Not needed. With your webshop connected, the agent looks up product information itself. Protocols are for processes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
