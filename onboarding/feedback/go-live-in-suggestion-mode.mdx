## Overview

In the Feedback step the agent works on real tickets while your team stays in control. It has three parts: this page (going live), [giving and processing feedback](/onboarding/feedback/give-and-process-feedback) and [automating your first topics](/onboarding/feedback/automate-your-first-topics).

{/* VIDEO: add the Loom embed for step 3, Feedback, here */}

**What you achieve on this page:** the agent writes a suggested reply on every new ticket in your helpdesk, and your team has access to give feedback.

**Time needed:** about 10 minutes.

## When are you ready?

When these three things are true:

- Your main protocols are published and set to **Active**.
- Your integrations are connected and tested.
- Your subscription is running.

You don't need everything in place. If your biggest topics are covered, usually order status, delivery and returns, you are ready. Live tickets give you many real examples every day, which the Playground cannot match.

<Warning>
  Don't go live with an empty Training Center. Your team will see suggestions that make no sense, and that first impression is hard to correct.
</Warning>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Organization** > **Members** and click **Invite**. Choose a role per colleague:

    - **Admin:** can see and change protocols. Give this to your owner.
    - **Support agent:** can give feedback on tickets, nothing else. Give this to everyone who handles tickets.

    There is no limit on the number of users. The insights page is behind the Minimal login, so everyone who gives feedback needs an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the agent">
    Go to [Agents](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/agents), open your agent, switch it from **paused** to **active** and click **Update**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the first suggestion">
    Send a test email to your support address and open the ticket in your helpdesk. Within a minute or two you see two internal notes: the suggested reply, and a link to the insights page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off other AI replies">
    If your helpdesk has its own AI agent or autoresponder that answers customers, switch it off once you are happy with Minimal's suggestions. Otherwise customers can get two different answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you activate

- **Nothing reaches a customer** unless you have switched on automation. Every reply is a suggestion that someone on your team reviews and sends.
- **Only new tickets** are picked up. Tickets already in your inbox stay with your team.
- **It works per agent**, so you can take one brand or country live and leave the others paused. You can pause the agent again at any time.
- **Tickets are counted** from now on. See [Start your subscription](/onboarding/prepare/start-your-subscription).

## What to expect in the first week

Mixed results, and that is normal. Your protocols cover the broad lines, but customers ask about the details and exceptions. You'll see three patterns:

- Topics handled well straight away, usually order status and tracking.
- Topics where one detail is consistently missing from the protocol.
- Tickets where the agent hands over because the protocol has no instruction for that case. That is correct behaviour, not a failure.

<Note>
  A reply like *"I'll check with the team and get back to you"* almost always means the agent has no information for that scenario. It won't invent an answer. Treat it as a sign that a protocol is missing or wasn't matched.
</Note>

<Check>
  **Done when:**

  - Your agent is **active** and new tickets get a suggestion and an insights link.
  - Your team has accounts, with one admin and support agents for everyone else.
</Check>

## Common pitfalls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No suggestions appear">
    Check that the agent is **active**, that the right channels are selected in the agent, and that your subscription is running. Only tickets that arrive after activating get a suggestion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Answering outside the helpdesk">
    Replies sent from Gmail or Outlook do not sync to the ticket, so the agent misses part of the conversation. Answer only in your helpdesk.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
