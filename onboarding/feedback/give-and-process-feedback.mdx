## Overview

The first jump in quality comes from your protocols. The last stretch, the details that make answers right every time, comes almost entirely from feedback. Every change you publish improves the very next ticket on that topic.

**What you achieve:** suggestions your team sends without changes on your main topics.

**Time needed:** about 15 minutes a day, roughly ten tickets, for one to two weeks.

## Before you start

- Your agent is active and writes suggestions. See [Go live in suggestion mode](/onboarding/feedback/go-live-in-suggestion-mode).
- Your team is invited, with one admin who processes feedback.

## Giving feedback on a suggestion

Every suggestion arrives as two internal notes in your helpdesk: the draft reply and a link to the insights page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Good? Copy and send">
    If the draft is right, copy it into your reply and send it. You don't need to mark it as good. Minimal measures copied suggestions automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wrong? Open the insights page">
    Click the link in the internal note. Log in to Minimal first if you are asked to. You see the reply, the explanation behind it and the AI Manager below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what was wrong and what it should have been">
    Both halves matter: *"It said 30 days. Our return window is 14 days."* Name the exact system and field where the right information is. Tone feedback counts too, such as *"we never open an email this way"*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or publish the change">
    Admins work through the change the AI Manager proposes and publish it. Support agents give a thumbs up or down with a short reason, which lands on the **Feedback** page for the admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Rewriting the draft yourself teaches the agent nothing. The ticket gets answered and the same mistake comes back tomorrow. Spend the extra ten seconds saying why.
</Warning>

## Processing your team's feedback

Feedback from colleagues only changes the agent once an admin processes it. Treat the **Feedback** page as a to do list and clear it every day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Feedback page">
    Go to [Feedback](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/feedback). It opens on negative feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Judge the feedback first">
    Not every piece of feedback is right. Decide what should change for future tickets, not only for this one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process it with the AI Manager">
    Click **Process with AI Manager**, or describe the change in your own words. Review the proposal and publish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as processed">
    Click **Mark as processed** at the bottom. The item leaves your list but isn't deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On a busy day, write a short note on the insights page and process your notes in one go at the end of the day. Five minutes every day works better than one long session a month.
</Tip>

<Check>
  **Done when:**

  - Your team gives feedback on suggestions every day, and the Feedback page is empty at the end of each day.
  - On your main topics, most suggestions are sent without changes.
</Check>

## Common pitfalls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Feedback that only says something is wrong">
    *"This is wrong"* needs a follow up conversation before anything can change. Say what the reply should have been.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not publishing the change">
    A proposal from the AI Manager does nothing until you click **Apply**, **Save** or **Publish**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Several people changing protocols">
    When everyone changes the knowledge, the tone and processes drift apart. Let the whole team give feedback and one admin process it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not sure why the agent answered like this">
    Ask the AI Manager on the insights page *"How did you arrive at this answer?"* It shows which protocol it followed and what information was missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Full guidance on writing usable feedback is in [Feedback](/training/feedback).
