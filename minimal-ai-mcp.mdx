## What the Minimal AI MCP is

The Minimal AI MCP is a [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server for your Minimal AI workspace. It lets the AI assistants you already work in, such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor, connect directly to your Minimal AI setup, so you can work on your agents and your support data without leaving the tool you're in.

It will serve two purposes:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A gateway to improve your AI Agents" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Review and refine how your [AI Agents](/set-up-and-integrations/ai-agents) behave: their [protocols](/training/protocols), [actions](/training/actions), and [automation](/training/automation) settings, from any MCP-compatible assistant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understand your support and insights" icon="chart-line">
    Ask questions about your tickets, [performance](/analytics), and trends, and get answers drawn from your live Minimal AI data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## A gateway to improve your AI Agents

The MCP brings the improvement loop you know from the [AI Manager](/ai-manager) into your own AI tools. You will be able to:

- **Diagnose a ticket.** Ask why a ticket was answered a certain way, drafted, escalated, or not automated.
- **Find what to fix.** Surface knowledge gaps, avoidable escalations, and automation opportunities from your recent tickets.
- **Propose changes.** Draft updates to protocols and agent instructions, so you can review them before they take effect.

<Note>
  Changes will follow the same safe review flow as in the dashboard: protocol edits are versioned, and nothing takes effect until you publish it.
</Note>

## Understand your support and insights

The MCP also turns your support data into something you can ask questions about, alongside your other work. You will be able to:

- **Review performance.** Pull your [direct resolution rate, automation rate, and billable tickets](/analytics) per agent and over time.
- **Spot trends.** See which topics customers are contacting you about, and how that changes week to week.
- **Combine context.** Bring Minimal AI insights into the same conversation as your other data and documents, for reporting or planning.
