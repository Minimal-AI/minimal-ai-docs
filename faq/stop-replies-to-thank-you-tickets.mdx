## Why this happens

By default your AI Agent tries to be helpful on every incoming message. Without specific guidance, that can mean it drafts or sends a reply to a customer's "thank you" or a ticket that's already resolved, when really no reply is needed at all.

## The fix is a protocol, not a Gorgias setting

This isn't something you configure in Gorgias — it's a training question for your agent. The best way to set it up is to describe the situation to the [AI Manager](/ai-manager):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Manager">
    Go to the [AI Manager](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/ai-manager) in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the scenario">
    Tell it something like _"Don't reply when a customer just says thanks or the ticket is already resolved."_ Be specific about what counts as 'finished' for your business — a short thank-you note, a closed ticket with no new question, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    The AI Manager will draft a protocol (or a rule) covering this and show you the proposed change. Review it, then publish it so the agent starts using it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This typically ends in a **silent escalation** or simply no action: the agent recognises the ticket doesn't need a reply and leaves it alone rather than sending something unnecessary. See [Escalating to a human](/training/protocols#escalating-to-a-human) for how that's structured inside a protocol.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Test the change in the [Playground](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/playground) with a few real thank-you or closed tickets before trusting it fully — it runs the same pipeline as a live ticket.
</Tip>
