## What happens when the agent escalates a ticket

When your AI Agent decides a ticket needs a human, it escalates it instead of guessing. In Gorgias this looks like:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Minimal adds an internal note">
    The agent posts an internal note on the ticket stating that Minimal escalated it. This note is only visible to your team, never to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The ticket stays open">
    Minimal leaves the ticket **open** in Gorgias so it doesn't get lost in a closed or resolved state. Your team picks it up from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Depending on how the protocol is written, an escalation can be **silent** (no message to the customer) or come with a short holding reply letting them know a specialist will follow up. Either way, the internal note and open status are the same. See [Escalating to a human](/training/protocols#escalating-to-a-human) for how this is configured.
</Note>

## Tracking escalated tickets in one place

Gorgias doesn't automatically group escalated tickets for you, but you can build your own view for it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a custom view in Gorgias">
    In Gorgias, create a new view (Settings \> Views, or from the sidebar \> Add view) and filter it on the internal note Minimal leaves, or on a tag if you've set one up for escalations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with your team">
    Make the view shared so everyone on your support team can see which tickets Minimal has handed off and pick them up quickly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure how to filter for it, or want the agent to tag escalations for easier filtering? Ask the [AI Manager](/ai-manager) — it can point you to the exact note text used, or help you adjust a protocol to also apply a tag on escalation.
</Tip>
