Connecting Zendesk lets your AI agents read and reply to tickets directly in your Zendesk helpdesk.

<Note>
  Zendesk has discontinued API token access for this integration. Connect with an OAuth client instead. Existing connections that still use an API token need to be reconnected.
</Note>

## What you'll need

- **Subdomain**: the `{subdomain}` in `https://{subdomain}.zendesk.com`.
- **OAuth Client Identifier**: the unique identifier of the OAuth client you create in Zendesk.
- **OAuth Client Secret**: the secret Zendesk shows after you save the OAuth client.
- **Sunshine Conversations App ID, Key ID and Secret** (optional): only needed if you use Zendesk's Sunshine Conversations messaging. If you provide one, provide all three.

## Create your Zendesk OAuth client

<Info>
  You need to be a Zendesk **administrator** to create an OAuth client.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    In Zendesk, click your **profile icon** in the top right of the screen, then click **Manage profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Admin Center">
    At the top left, click **View in Admin Center**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open OAuth Clients">
    Go to **Apps and integrations \> APIs \> OAuth Clients**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a client">
    Click **Add OAuth client**, then fill in a **Client name** and a **Description** (for example, `Minimal AI`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the client kind">
    Set **Client kind** to **Confidential**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and copy your credentials">
    Click **Save**. Zendesk now reveals the **Secret**. Copy both the **Unique identifier** and the **Secret**, and store them somewhere safe. Zendesk only shows the full secret once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect in Minimal AI

Go to [Dashboard \> Integrations](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/integrations), click **Add an integration \> Zendesk**, and fill in your subdomain, **OAuth Client Identifier** and **OAuth Client Secret**. Leave the Sunshine fields blank unless you use Sunshine Conversations.

<Warning>
  Never share your OAuth client secret with third parties. Only paste it into Minimal AI's Zendesk integration.
</Warning>
