---
title: "Commslayer"
description: "Connect Commslayer to Minimal AI"
---

Connecting Commslayer lets your AI agents answer the email conversations that **you** send them. You decide which conversations reach Minimal AI with one rule in Commslayer, for example every new customer message, or only the conversations Commslayer's own AI could not solve.

It takes about 10 minutes.

## How it works

- **Your rule** sends a conversation to Minimal AI. Minimal AI never watches the whole inbox.
- **Minimal AI** reads the conversation, answers in Commslayer, and escalates to your team when it cannot solve it.
- **Commslayer's own AI** keeps working as before. Minimal AI waits while Commslayer's AI is still handling a conversation.

## What you'll need

- A Commslayer account with **admin** rights (needed for integration tokens and rules).
- An email inbox connected in Commslayer.

## Step 1: Create an integration token

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration tokens">
    In Commslayer, open **Settings > Integration tokens** and create a token named `Minimal AI`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the permissions">
    Enable **Conversations** (read and write), **Messages** (read and write), **Contacts** (read and write), **Webhooks** (manage) and **Reports** (read).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the token">
    Copy the token when Commslayer shows it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Commslayer tokens expire 90 days after they are created. Before that date, create a new token and paste it under **Replace integration token** on the Commslayer connection in Minimal AI.
</Note>

## Step 2: Connect in Minimal AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the integration">
    Go to [Dashboard \> Integrations](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/integrations), click **Add an integration \> Commslayer**, paste your token and click **Connect Commslayer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose inboxes and escalation">
    Select the email inboxes Minimal AI may answer. Under **Escalate to**, choose the teammate who receives conversations Minimal AI cannot solve, or keep **Unassigned human inbox**. Click **Connect Commslayer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an agent">
    Open the Commslayer connection and assign a published, active AI agent to each inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook URL">
    In the same connection, copy the **Webhook URL**. You need it in the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Treat the webhook URL like a password. Anyone with it can send conversations to Minimal AI. Only paste it into your Commslayer rule.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Create a rule in Commslayer

Open **Automations** in Commslayer and create a rule that ends with the action **Send webhook event**, using your webhook URL. Choose the trigger that matches how you want to work:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Answer every new customer message">
    - **Event**: *Message created*
    - **Conditions**: *Message type* is incoming, and *Inbox* is your support inbox
    - **Action**: *Send webhook event*, with your webhook URL
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Answer only after Commslayer's AI hands over">
    1. In your Commslayer AI agent's handover settings, choose the teammate the conversation is assigned to when the AI hands over.
    2. Create the rule:
       - **Event**: *Conversation updated*
       - **Conditions**: *Assignee* is that teammate, and *Inbox* is your support inbox
       - **Action**: *Send webhook event*, with your webhook URL
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Make sure the rule is on the **Active** tab. To stop Minimal AI, switch the rule off in Commslayer.

## Step 4: Test it

1. Send a test email to the inbox from a mailbox you control, and let it reach your rule.
2. Within a minute Minimal AI answers in the Commslayer conversation. If your agent is in review mode, it adds its suggested answer as a private note instead.
3. Reply as a teammate in the same conversation to confirm Minimal AI does not answer it again.

## Good to know

- **Email only.** Chat, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram conversations are not handled yet.
- **Escalation.** When Minimal AI cannot solve a conversation it adds a private note, opens the conversation and assigns it to your escalation teammate (or leaves it unassigned). It does not answer that conversation again.
- **Teammates take over.** Once a teammate has replied, Minimal AI stays out.
- **Files.** Minimal AI reads files customers send. Its replies cannot include files.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Commslayer rejected the token">
    Check that the token has all five permissions from Step 1 and has not expired, then paste it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No inbox appears">
    Only email inboxes are listed. Finish connecting the inbox in Commslayer first, and make sure the token has **Reports** (read).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Minimal AI does not answer">
    Check that the rule is active and uses the exact webhook URL from the connection, that the inbox in the rule is one you connected, and that a published, active agent is assigned to that inbox. If Commslayer's own AI is still handling the conversation, Minimal AI waits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need a new webhook URL">
    Disconnect the Commslayer connection in Minimal AI and connect it again. Then update your rule with the new URL.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
