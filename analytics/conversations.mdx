## Overview

The **Conversations** page lets you read individual customer conversations together with Minimal's analysis of them: how the conversation ended, who answered, why it was escalated and which protocols were used. Use it to check the stories behind the trends on [Insights](/analytics/insights).

You'll find it under **Analytics** at [gominimal.ai/dashboard/conversations](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/conversations). It covers the **last 30 days**, newest first.

The page has three panes:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="List" icon="list">
    Every conversation, with its status, who answered and whether it was escalated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transcript" icon="messages">
    The full read-only conversation, including drafts and internal notes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Details" icon="circle-info">
    The verdict, escalation, protocols, lookups and timing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Finding a conversation

- **Search ticket id**: type a ticket id and press Enter. Only exact matches are returned.
- **Filters**: narrow the list by **Language**, **Status**, **Sentiment**, **Who answered**, **Escalated** and **Protocol**. Each option shows how many conversations it matches.
- **Show**: switch between **Minimal took part** and **All messages**, and between **Tickets** and **Storefront**.

Use **↑ / ↓** (or **j / k**) to move through the list, and **Esc** to step back.

## Reading the list

Each row has a status dot, the subject, the time since the last message and a **who answered** chip:

| Chip | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI only** | Only the AI replied |
| **AI assisted** | A human sent the reply, working from a draft the AI wrote |
| **Mixed** | Both the AI and a human replied |
| **Human only** | Only humans replied |
| **No reply** | Nobody replied to the customer |

An **Escalated** chip means the AI decided a person was needed in this conversation.

## Reading a conversation

The transcript shows each message with its author: **Customer**, your agent, or **Human agent**. Chips below a message show what happened behind it, for example **Draft · never sent**, **Escalated**, **From AI draft**, **Internal note**, or the protocol and lookups that were used.

The **Details** panel summarises the whole conversation:

- **Verdict**: status, sentiment and who answered.
- **Escalation**: why the AI handed off, with the instruction or protocol behind it.
- **Protocols**: those exposed to the agent, used by the agent, and used by the human.
- **Lookups & actions**, **Tags & topics** and **Timing**.

Click any message to see its own details, such as reply latency, language and tool calls. For AI replies, **See detailed feedback** opens the full analysis of that reply.

<Tip>
  An escalation's cause tells you what to fix. For example, **No protocol coverage** points to a missing [protocol](/training/protocols), and **No action connected** to a missing [action](/training/actions).
</Tip>

<Note>
  Conversations are kept for 30 days. Older messages show **This message is no longer retained.**
</Note>
