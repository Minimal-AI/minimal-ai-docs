## Overview

The **Conversations** page shows individual conversations from the last 30 days, together with Minimal's reading of each one. Use it to check the stories behind the trends on [Insights](/analytics/insights), or to find out why a specific ticket went the way it did.

You'll find it under **Analytics** at [gominimal.ai/dashboard/conversations](https://gominimal.ai/dashboard/conversations). To find a ticket, search for its id or filter the list.

[Insights](/analytics/insights) explains what a conversation is and what its **status**, **sentiment**, **topics** and **tags** mean. This page covers what you see inside a conversation.

## Who answered

Every conversation is described by who replied to the customer:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI only** | Only the AI replied. No human touched the conversation |
| **AI assisted** | A human sent the reply, working from a draft the AI wrote. The customer only saw the human's message, but the AI did the first pass |
| **Mixed** | The AI replied, and a human was also involved |
| **Human only** | Only humans replied. The AI wrote nothing, not even a draft |
| **No reply** | Nothing reached the customer. An AI draft that nobody sent counts here too |

## Messages

The transcript shows everything on the thread, including messages the customer never saw:

- **Sent reply**: a message the customer received.
- **Draft**: a reply the AI wrote for your team to review. **From AI draft** marks a human message that started as one.
- **Internal note**: a note visible only to your team.

Click any message to see its details, such as how long the reply took, its language, and the lookups and actions the agent ran. For AI replies, **See detailed feedback** opens the full analysis of that reply.

## Escalations

A conversation is **escalated** when the AI decides a person is needed. It can say so in a reply, in a draft, or silently, by handing off without telling the customer.

Every escalation gets a **cause**, which tells you what, if anything, to change:

| Cause | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your instructions said to**: a protocol, an operational rule or your tone of voice required a person | Nothing. The agent did what you asked. Review the instruction if you want these automated |
| **No protocol covered it** | Write a [protocol](/training/protocols) for the request |
| **No action could do it**: the reply would have promised a change no connected action can make | Connect an [action](/training/actions) |
| **Customer asked for a person**, or **Minimal's safety rules** required one, for example a serious health issue | Nothing. These should stay human |
| **A lookup or action failed**, or the system forced the hand-off | Nothing on your side. Minimal fixes these |

The escalation panel quotes the instruction or protocol behind the hand-off, so you can go straight to it.

The cause is an AI assessment. Read it as a trend across many conversations, not as an exact verdict on any single one.

## Protocols

A conversation lists [protocols](/training/protocols) in three ways:

- **Exposed to the agent**: protocols the agent was shown for a reply. It sees at most 20 per reply.
- **Used by the agent**: protocols it actually followed.
- **Used by the human**: protocols your team followed in their replies.

A protocol that is exposed but rarely used may need a clearer description. A request your team keeps handling without a protocol is a candidate for a new one.

<Note>
  Conversations are kept for 30 days after their last message. Older messages show **This message is no longer retained.**
</Note>
